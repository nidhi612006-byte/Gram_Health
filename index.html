Life-Saving First Aid

Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) Ratio Guide

Dehydration from severe diarrhea or vomiting is dangerous, especially in young children. If commercial WHO-ORS packets are not immediately reachable, prepare clean homemade rehydration fluid:

Liter(s)
💧 1 Liter Boiled & Cooled Water
🥄 6 Teaspoons Clean Sugar (चीनी)
🧂 1/2 Teaspoon Common Salt (नमक)

⚠️ Do NOT add excess salt or artificial color. Stir until fully dissolved. Give in small sips. Always visit the PHC if vomiting continues or stool has blood.
